# WallpaperLive icon sources

The project author selected **A · Jendela senja** as the final icon on 9 November 2026. The original illustration depicts a tilted graphite window, copper edges, a cream sunset, and a winding light path through an orange canyon. It uses the application's cream, ink, and burnt-orange palette with warm shading for depth.

## Shipped assets

- [wallpaperlive.svg](wallpaperlive.svg): editable vector source; no external image, font, or script. SHA256: `a5cebbfde0dc6de5bb4b1b001b1e7ac01ced80190cdcbdc24b225eb8e8827e9f`.
- [wallpaperlive.png](wallpaperlive.png): transparent 512 × 512 PNG, rendered from the SVG at 2× resolution using Chromium and downsampled with Pillow. The native app embeds this image for window and tray icons. SHA256: `cbc08f661ef21af58736157a2e9342cd52a7075655cc0bcd760cbe3395127279`.
- [wallpaperlive.ico](wallpaperlive.ico): transparent frames at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixels for executable shell icons, Setup, shortcuts, and uninstall display. SHA256: `ba13b4ff5290afb7c41f12260a0d156dcb8befa6cf8e049383ae7ab10952535f`.

## References, not copied artwork

- [Microsoft app-icon guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/style/iconography/app-icon-design): clear metaphor and small-size silhouette.
- [Iconfactory's Marta case study](https://design.iconfactory.com/marta-app-icon/): product behavior informs the form.
- [Screen Studio brand materials](https://screen.studio/brand-materials): connected form and deliberate material depth.

These sources informed the review; no third-party logo or wallpaper image was used in the icon. Existing performance screenshots retain their original artwork and version labels; the new logo does not turn them into v0.1.7 measurements.
